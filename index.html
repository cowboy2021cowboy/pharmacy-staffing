How to assign and adjust

  1. Type the queue at 7:00 (pending + processing + verified-not-packed).
  2. Put Early heads on Pens and DRG first. Cold must clear by 4pm.
  3. Put remaining Early and Mid on ambient and production.
  4. Watch Done 7:00. Cold done after 16:00 means move people now.
  5. At 11:00 retype the remaining queue and move heads.
  6. Associates do Spark labeling, help RPh Spark, pen line (3 FA per tech), then prepack.

Pen line rate = tech pen rate ÷ 3. Changes sync about every 8 seconds when the board is connected.
